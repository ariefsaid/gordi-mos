Orient Work Ops Money

4 tabs clears the high-schooler

Orient · Work · Ops · Money is one over A's three, but each is a distinct heavy rhythm a director can name. Four is still inside the working-memory limit. Inbox is a bell in the topbar — it never competes for tab real estate. Compare with variant C's cramped 5-tab bar.

✓ Why this spine wins — & the honest risk

Safer for heavy money/ops users

Finance and the floor get real destinations, not folded panels. The owner's money cockpit is full and heavy — AR is a worked queue with lifecycle, not a summary card. Ops owns the roast log and kitchen plan as a place, not a relay.

Inbox stops being a home

Demoting inbox to a bell + slide-over kills the "live in your inbox" disease. It's a triage router: every item routes to Orient/Work/Ops/Money. You land in a destination, not a queue.

Kitchen sprawl solved locally

Roastery & Kitchen are an Activity switcher under Ops, with a LOCAL module nav (Log · Plan · Stock · Review). The old 5-primary-item kitchen sprawl collapses into one scoped place — no rail bloat.

4 tabs still clears the high-schooler

Orient · Work · Ops · Money is one over A's three, but each is a distinct heavy rhythm a director can name. Four is still inside the working-memory limit, and each tab earns its place.

Orient stays light by design

Orient summarizes and drills — it never duplicates Money/Ops detail. The attention strip routes you out; the summary rows hand off to destinations. This is the discipline that keeps 4 tabs from becoming 5 panels.

The honest risk

Four destinations demand a shared grammar: Work's task lifecycle, Money's invoice lifecycle, and Ops's log/approval lifecycle must feel like one system or the app reads as four mini-apps. Unify the pills, drawers, and drill conventions — or B fragments where A folds.

Open questions for Arief
  1. 4 vs 3 destinations. Does Money + Ops as first-class earn their tab, or does the owner still think "money lives in Orient"? If finance is a daily-driver, B wins; if money is a weekly glance, A's fold is lighter.
  2. Inbox as bell — where does triage live? The slide-over routes to destinations, but do fast triage actions (snooze, assign) belong in the slide-over, or must they always force a destination hop? Routing-only vs light-acting is the call.
  3. Ops grammar unifying with Work. Roast "Submitted → Approved" mirrors task "Open → Done". Should Ops Review reuse Work's approval drawer, or stay a module-local queue? Same question for Money's "settled requires evidence".
  4. Orient drill vs Orient dump. B's Orient must stay a summary that hands off. If role-scoping is lazy, Orient re-becomes a dashboard and B loses its reason to exist over A. Discipline test, not structure test.
