? What this tests

One variable: warmth.

DESIGN.md's Quiet Control Surface is white-on-near-white with one blue. It reads precise and deliberate — the question is whether "precise" reads as clinical to a 30-person team that opens this app every morning. This probe holds everything constant — IA (Orient · Work · Inbox), layout, data, the One Blue Rule, the Tinted-Status palette, shadow system, geometry — and changes only the warmth of the surfaces, text, navy and shadow tint. Nothing structural moves. If the warmer skin wins, the change is a token-only amendment, not a redesign.

The question

Is the quiet control surface too clinical for a company that lives in this app daily? A roastery team, a floor operator logging roasts, a BU-head chasing invoices — do they want a tool that feels like a precision instrument, or one that feels like a warm workspace? Restraint can read as cold when it's the only surface you see for 8 hours.

What changed (token-only)

Surfaces shift from neutral grey 240° to cream ~38–40°. Navy softens 218 → 210°, lower saturation. Text becomes a warm near-black 30 8% 12%. The One Blue nudges 221 → 225° (barely). Shadow tint follows the warmer navy. Status colors untouched.

What did NOT change

The 10 Named Rules. The One Blue is still the only saturated interactive. Tinted-Status is intact. Single-Border holds. Structural-Navy carries weight, never action. Orange stays a ≤2 sprinkle. Soft-Elevation, No-Pure-Black, Tabular-Numbers, Mono-IDs — all preserved. Same IA, same screens, same Gordi data.

The risk of warmth

Cream surfaces can soften contrast and reduce the "instrument" precision that makes numbers feel authoritative — relevant for a money cockpit. The warmer navy at 210° can pull slightly purple in some displays. If warmth costs legibility on the AR-overdue figure or the dense Work table, the quiet surface was right.

If Arief prefers the Warmer skin → the warmer token block above becomes a DESIGN.md amendment proposal. Because nothing structural changed, the amendment is a :root token swap — the 10 Named Rules, the component classes, and every screen built on base.css inherit the warmer skin with zero rework. Open Variant A side-by-side and decide: quiet instrument, or warm workspace?
