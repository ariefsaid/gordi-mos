Option B - work-led

The app opens on Work. Objectives and tasks become the main spine, while money and ops feed exceptions into it.

This is the critical alternative if the true job is management cadence, not monitoring. There is no Dashboard and no Home. The first screen is a queue of commitments, risks, owners, and objective context.

What this kills

  • NowDemoted to a saved Work view named Needs attention.
  • CascadeReplaced by Objective detail and Task roll-up context.
  • DashboardOnly appears as Money summaries embedded in work exceptions.
  • KeptWeekly updates, follow-ups, task ownership, objectives, and RACI, but with simpler placement.

Best-practice argument

Work-management tools often center owned work rather than analytics. This reduces landing-page complexity and turns every metric into a task, follow-up, or objective signal.

Critical risk

If MOS also needs to be the owner's fast operating cockpit, this option can feel too queue-heavy and too manager-centric. It may bury pure monitoring jobs.
